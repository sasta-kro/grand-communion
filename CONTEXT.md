# Communion

Communion hosts shared fictional scenes where characters converse or sustain activities in their own world. A person outside that world observes and may contribute through identities inside it.

## Language

**Scene**:
The shared fictional place whose public speech and events are available to its characters, such as a classroom, tavern, cave, or council hall.
_Avoid_: hall as a universal name, channel, workspace

**Scenario**:
A reusable starting setup describing the setting, cast, shared opening premise, and optional ongoing activity. Starting the same scenario again creates another instance of its world.

**Session**:
One continuing instance of a scenario, with its own characters, history, memories, and unfolding activities.

**Save**:
A recoverable point in a session that preserves its world and characters as they have evolved, rather than merely preserving the starting scenario.

**Persona**:
A reusable definition of personality, values, voice, and dispositions that can be assigned to multiple characters.
_Avoid_: agent, bot, soul

**Character**:
An individual within the fiction, with an identity, a persona, personal history, and relationships. Characters sharing a persona remain distinct people in the fiction.
_Avoid_: API instance, account

**Councillor**:
A character occupying a recognized seat or position in a council. A councillor is one possible character role rather than a requirement of every hall.

**Ambient voice**:
A contribution from surrounding people, with attribution appropriate to the scenario, such as a bystander, crowd, audience member, or anonymous voice. Multiple kinds may coexist, and anonymous contributions do not automatically represent one recurring individual.
_Avoid_: audience voice as a universal display label

**Operator**:
The person observing and configuring Communion from outside the fiction. Characters have no inherent knowledge of the operator or of which contributions the operator controls.
_Avoid_: supreme leader, in-world user

**Utterance**:
A publicly audible piece of speech, which may address everyone or particular characters. Addressing someone does not make the utterance private; returning no response produces no utterance.

**World event**:
An occurrence within the fiction, distinct from something a character merely claims happened.

**Activity**:
What characters are engaged in, such as debate, conversation, chanting, prayer, or another continuing undertaking. An activity need not have a completion condition.

**Observation**:
An utterance or event available to a particular character at a particular point in the session.

**Memory**:
A character's first-person retained account of earlier observations and experiences. A memory may contain that character's beliefs without establishing those beliefs as facts about the world.

**No response**:
A character's explicit choice not to contribute speech on its current turn. Hearing the scene is automatic and does not require any listening action.

**Narrator**:
An observer that recounts the session for the operator in a configurable narrative voice. Its account does not become speech, character memory, or an event in the fictional world.

**Recap**:
A narrator's account of a specified span of the session, preserving what was observed, claimed, or disputed. A recap may identify notable moments without treating its interpretations as established world facts.
